ReDraw <Anime Characters>

Over the past two years, I’ve been nurturing my art skills through redrawing—and occasionally tracing—as a way to learn and grow. Lately, I’ve also been exploring and embracing the journey of finding my own unique art style.

Redraw is when artists take an existing image—like a screenshot or old drawing—and recreate it in their own style. It’s a fun way to practice skills like anatomy, coloring, or expressions. Many artists use it to show progress or add creative twists to fanart. Sharing redraws online often boosts engagement and helps build an art portfolio. It’s not just copying—it’s learning, improving, and making it yours.

My Deviations

A “Deviation” on DeviantArt is any creative piece those are uploaded — whether it’s digital art, traditional sketches, photography, or any others artworks. It’s an artist's personal expression shared with the community, each one reflecting the artist's unique style and artistic journey.

I’ve gently stepped into the world of DeviantArt as a growing artist, hoping to share pieces of my imagination and heart through every stroke. This space is a little garden for my progress—where I bloom slowly, learn openly, and connect with kindred creatives. Thank you for stopping by and walking this journey with me.

DeviantArt is a social network that launched in August 2000, creating a space for artists and art enthusiasts to showcase their incredible work, find inspiration, connect with others, and even sell their creations. It boasts over 100 million registered members worldwide, hosting more than 650 million pieces of art. Plus, tens of thousands of original pieces—everything from painting and sculpture to digital art, pixel art, films, and anime—are uploaded every single day.

Creative Challenges

A DeviantArt Creative Challenge is a themed art event where Deviants or artists create and share artworks based on a specific theme or set of rules.

These time-limited challenges spark creativity, help to explore new styles, and connect with the art community. They're a fun way to grow your skills, earn badges, and sometimes win cool prizes or cash!
